Okay, so what exactly is DLS?

Dirty Little Secrets is a college-era alternate universe about public identities, private feelings, terrible timing, and a social circle operating with approximately forty percent of the available information.

Everybody is older. Everybody is technically responsible for their own choices. This has not improved the decision-making.

Why is it called “Dirty Little Secrets”?

posted: fall semester, 2006

Because the story is built on the distance between what is true and what everybody thinks is true.

Aaliyah and Tamaki are together. That part is not complicated. The complicated part is that their friends possess several completely accurate pieces of information and repeatedly fail to assemble them into one obvious conclusion.

Other secrets are smaller: a crush admitted to the wrong friend, an ex everybody assumes is still an option, a “platonic” Top 8 placement that keeps moving upward, or a profile song selected with far too much intention.

Current feelings are not narrative destiny. Public knowledge is not objective truth.
